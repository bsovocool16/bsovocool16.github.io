Should Lawyers Deregulate?

2026-08-27

At a high level, we lawyers have done pretty well for ourselves as a group. Law firm revenues and fees have increased steadily year-over-year, while top lawyers command ever-higher pay packages. Graduates from top law schools can expect a starting salary of $235,000, plus $20,000 in hours-based bonuses.[FN: Not including a $6,000 special bonus for 2026.] 

It’s difficult to exhaustively tally the factors that have led to the spectacular success of the legal industry, but an easy place to start is the broad prohibition on the practice of law without a license. In most jurisdictions, this rule also prohibits the ownership of law firms by nonlawyers.

Bar licensure, the bar exam, and their virtues and vices are widely discussed in the legal industry and the points are well-trodden on each side. Advocates say the bar imposes a minimum standard of competency for lawyers, is a quality indicator for law schools, and provides a means of discipline for bad lawyers.
