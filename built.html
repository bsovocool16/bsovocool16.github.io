things i've built

Tools and evaluations I've made that you can install, run or read.

grading legal judgment against real outcomes

  • legal-evals — legal reasoning evaluations graded against logged future outcomes: two Harbor RL environments (merger-proxy fairness sections, patent claim amendments), a log-loss forecasting ledger on patent examination, and screens for errors in SEC filings.

tools for practitioners

  • fairness-opinion-legal — a Claude plugin that drafts the “Opinion of [Financial Advisor]” section of a merger proxy from the advisor’s own EDGAR precedents, with a redline for the reviewer.
  • filetree-coverage — you know what your agent saw, but you don’t know what it didn’t see. I made a Claude plugin that records which files in a data room a session actually searched, opened and relied on, and draws the result as a nifty coverage map.
  • deck-time — Stream Deck overlay for Intapp Time. Runs locally with time entry controls, dictation, auto-formatting, and other QOL features.

for fun

  • Seal the Deal — a browser game about fairness opinions. You play a banking analyst with until the 7:00 AM board call to get a bad price approved as fair, by working the WACC, the comps and the add-backs before the committee’s smell test catches you. Source.
