things i've built

Tools and evaluations I've made that you can install, run or read. Everything here is built from public filings and records. Each tool's output is a draft for attorney review, not legal advice.

grading legal judgment against real outcomes

  • legal-evals — evaluations that grade legal reasoning against outcomes models can’t have seen: two Harbor RL environments (merger-proxy fairness sections, patent claim amendments), a log-loss forecasting ledger on patent examination, and screens for errors in SEC filings.

tools for practitioners

  • fairness-opinion-legal — a Claude plugin that drafts the “Opinion of [Financial Advisor]” section of a merger proxy from the advisor’s own EDGAR precedents, with a redline for the reviewer.
  • filetree-coverage — a Claude plugin that records which files in a data room a session actually searched, opened and relied on, and draws the result as a coverage map.
  • deck-time — matter timers on a Stream Deck, billing narratives drafted without an AI model, local dictation, and one-click export to Intapp Time. Runs entirely on your own computer.

in progress

  • A blinded study of whether models can forecast FDA complete response letters from the legal standard and the public record, scored against base rates and market prices.
  • A study of whether the cases that go to trial teach us the law, using post-Alice patent rulings. Background in legal superintelligence and volatility harvesting.
  • The structure of every credit agreement filed on EDGAR since 2001. Background in what i’m working on.
